HOW TO USE

Export from iSAMS, then import here

The screenshots below show the exact iPad flow used for the demonstration PDF.

4 STEPS
1

Open Timetable

In the pupil portal, tap Timetable in the top navigation.

2

Open My Timetable

Choose My Timetable, then wait for the full Monday–Friday grid to load.

3

Share the PDF

Tap the browser share icon, select Pupil Portal | My Timetable, then choose Save to Files.

4

Save and import

Save the file somewhere easy to find, return here, and tap Import PDF / JSON.

STEP 1 · IMPORT

Bring in your timetable

Open Import, choose the PDF exported from iSAMS or a compatible JSON file, and wait for the import confirmation.

PDF rows are read into the Campus grid. Reg–P12 are always available; P13/P14 stay blank when the source does not contain them.

STEP 2 · CHECK

Review the timetable

Open Timetable to check each day, period, teacher and room. Tap a lesson cell to edit it in Cell Editor.

Use Load demo to inspect the layout before importing your own data.

STEP 3 · BATCH ENTRY

Fill several cells at once

Open Batch entry, select the required cells, enter the lesson details once, then choose Apply to selected.

Use Mark selected cells as standing lessons when the lesson should remain a regular weekly entry.

STEP 4 · FUN TIMETABLE

Preview a different arrangement

Open Fun Timetable, choose an option, and select Generate option to see a simulated timetable.

This page is for comparison only. It never changes your real timetable and has no download action.

STEP 5 · EXPORT

Create a device wallpaper

Open Export, choose your device and timetable format, check the preview, then choose Download PNG.

Use Native timetable · Reg–P12 for the school-only format, or Campus + P13/P14 when you want the extended evening rows.

Demo: tap Load demo at the top to see a neutral sample timetable before importing your own file. PDF recognition keeps Campus through P12, preserves optional P13/P14 when present, and ignores rows after P14.
